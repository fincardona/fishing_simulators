🎣 Trolling spread simulator

Interactive simulator for visualizing how fishing lines spread behind a boat while trolling.

This web simulator lets you configure the number of rods, line length, line diameter, lure weight, rod position, trolling speed and sea current. The simulation shows in real time how the lines behave while the boat moves or changes course.

It is designed as a practical visual tool for planning a trolling spread, understanding lure separation, reducing the risk of crossing lines and observing the effect of turns and current. The model is simplified, but it accounts for boat speed, current, line drag, lure drag, lure mass and rod layout.
